Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam

Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam (1931–2015) was one of India's most respected scientists and the 11th President of India.

Early Life

Kalam was born in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu.

Interesting Facts

• Water is written as H2O.
• Einstein's famous equation is E = mc2.
• Kalam always encouraged students to dream big and work hard.
